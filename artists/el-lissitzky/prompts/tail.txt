TAIL — geometry and the negatives, identical for every work:
The image is the whole sheet or canvas, edge to edge, photographed flat, nothing else in the picture. No frame, no mat, no wall, no gallery. Do not copy the composition of any attached reference; compose something new in its language. No glossy 3D rendering, no blurred drop shadows, no digital gradients, no vanishing-point perspective, no faces with features. No signature (the real sheets carry a small pencil signature lower right; this one must not), no monogram, no edition number, no collector's stamp, no inventory number, no watermark, no logo.
