# El Lissitzky — the measured visual language

Measured 2026-11-20 off Credo No. 1's six works plus two more *Victory over the Sun* sheets from Commons.
Only works of 1930 and earlier are used anywhere in this repo (US public domain).

- **Three bodies of work, three looks.**
  - *Prouns* (1920–24): beams, slabs, discs and arcs floating at angles, in parallel (axonometric) projection with
    no vanishing point, on a pale, mottled, textured ground. Matte black, warm greys, cream, ochre; red at most a
    small accent. Some planes carry real texture (sand, stipple, wood grain). Thin rods act as tension wires.
  - *Victory over the Sun* figurines (1923 lithographs): one machine-puppet built of a quarter-disc, squares,
    cylinders and thin black arcs, standing on a broad white sheet. Printer's red, dense black, graphite grey smudged
    into gradation, a little yellow or slate blue.
  - *Posters and covers* (1919–23): flat red, black and paper white, one hard diagonal or circular division, one
    driving form (the wedge, the circle), scattered bars like debris, type used as shape.
- **Composition is off-centre, with empty ground around it.** Shadows are flat grey shapes, never soft.
- **Never:** perspective with a vanishing point, glossy volume, faces with features, legible words when generating.
- **Trap:** the figurine sheets carry his small pencil signature lower right. Nothing generated in this repo may
  reproduce it.
