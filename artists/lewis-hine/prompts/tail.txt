TAIL — geometry and the negatives, identical for every photograph:
The image is the photograph itself, filling the frame edge to edge: no white print border, no black film border, no rebate, no sprocket holes, no mount, no frame. Black and white only, no colour, no sepia toning. Do not copy the scene or the people of any attached reference; compose something new in its language. Nothing modern: no hard hats, no high-visibility vests, no safety harnesses, no plastic, no phones, no modern cars or towers. No caption, no handwritten or scratched negative number, no archive stamp, no signature, no watermark, no logo.
