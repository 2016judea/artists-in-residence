TAIL — geometry and the negatives, identical for every page:
The image is the whole page, edge to edge: a real printed or manuscript leaf of old laid paper photographed flat, its own edges visible, nothing else in the picture. No mount, no frame, no table, no hands, no modern objects. Do not copy the figure or subject of any attached reference; compose something new in its language. No colour beyond the ink and the paper. No arrowheads (rays are plain lines), no modern diagram symbols, no printed body text, no running head, no title, no caption, no page number, no signature, no library stamp, no shelfmark, no inventory number, no watermark, no logo.
