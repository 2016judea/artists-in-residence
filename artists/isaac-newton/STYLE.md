# Isaac Newton — the measured visual language

Measured 2026-11-20 off the sheets in `sheets/` (Aetas Nova No. 2's art, plus his Organon notebook from Commons).
The prompts in `prompts/` paraphrase this. Provenance: `sheets/CREDITS.md`.

- **Two hands, never mixed.** The engraver's (Opticks 1704, Principia 1687, *System of the World* 1728) and his own
  (notebooks, c. 1661–65).
- **Engraved plates are sheets of demonstrations.** Several unrelated figures of different sizes float on one plate
  with bare paper between them and no boxes. Each carries a small italic engraved "Fig. N".
- **Line is hairline, ruled and uniform.** Rays are straight ruled lines with no arrowheads; circles are compass
  work; lenses are clean lens outlines; prisms are triangles in plain perspective; a candle on a stand is the source.
- **Tone only where the physics has it.** A band of colours is a field of very fine parallel lines; rings and the
  rainbow are concentric stippled arcs; a globe gets curved hatching; a comet's tail is a sheaf of parallel strokes.
- **Points are lettered.** Engraved capitals (A B C D…), a few lowercase italics.
- **The notebook page is mostly writing.** Small, fast, slanting brown-ink hand edge to edge, paragraph numbers in
  the margin, strikeouts, one small exact sketch set into the text (snowflake crystals, a ray diagram).
- **Never:** colour, ornament, people, pictorial scenes, arrowheads, a frame around a figure.
- **Traps:** the Opticks plates and the comet in `sheets/` were negated back to ink-on-paper from the issue's
  white-on-black stencils. Never `flip`: the letters reverse.
